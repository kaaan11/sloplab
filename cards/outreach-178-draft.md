## Draft comment

Thank you for collecting maintainer guidance here. I would like to offer a small, optional appendix of
worked examples for the evidence-handling part of the draft, and to ask whether it fills a gap before
building anything larger.

Each example is a short, fully synthetic report with an explicit fictional program policy. It separates
three questions that often get merged in triage: what the supplied evidence supports, whether the issue is
in scope under that policy, and what a human should do next. The next step is one of three: *verify* a
named claim, *request specific information* that could change the decision, or flag the report as *likely
out of scope*. Confidence is ordinal (low / medium / high), not a probability.

Three samples are drafted: a valid core finding with an unsupported impact extension, a terse but
sufficient report, and an accurate observation about an excluded asset.

Limits, stated up front: the examples are synthetic, model-assisted and owner-adjudicated; they make no
claim about real-world prevalence, production accuracy, AI authorship or time saved. "Verify" means a claim
is ready for human checking, not that a vulnerability is confirmed.

Would two or three such examples be useful as an appendix, and which distinction would you keep?
