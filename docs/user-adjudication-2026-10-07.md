# User adjudication — 7 October 2026

The user accepted four source-aware assistant proposals after comparing the
original inputs, rubric and two human explanations. This is an assisted user
judgment, not a third blind independent rating or a replacement for the original
human responses. Only the
[aggregate sidecar](../experiments/results/human-review/adjudication-2026-10-07/summary.json)
is public; private inputs and the full accepted decision record remain local.

Two claim-status disagreements were decided: one `missing`, one `contradictory`.
The distinction is between absent evidence of an effect and supplied information
that contradicts a specific claim about the evidence. The original first/second
human claim agreement remains 16/18; it was not recalculated as agreement with
this later user judgment.

Both reviewed report pairs have a meaningful quality change. Their action-change
axes were accepted as `uncertain`: the missing procedural steps do not, by
themselves, establish different rubric actions for the two texts. Further evidence
would be required for a definite yes/no judgment. This is an explicit disposition
of uncertainty, not proof that the historical decision-preserving targets are
correct or incorrect.

Original response sheets, model votes, authored targets and historical summaries
are preserved. No corpus relabeling or new model calls were part of this
adjudication. New independently authored private inputs, additional blind raters
and broader validation remain separate future work.
